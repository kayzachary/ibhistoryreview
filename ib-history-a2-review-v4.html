Teacher Directions ▼
  1. Pick teams. Divide the class into three teams of roughly equal size. Have each team choose a name or simply use Team 1, 2, 3. Designate one student per team as the "captain" who will deliver the team's final answer.
  2. Choose who goes first. Use a quick method — coin flip, rock-paper-scissors, or ask a warm-up question and award first pick to the fastest correct response.
  3. Selecting a question. The team in control picks a category and point value from the board. Click the corresponding cell to reveal the question.
  4. Answering the question. Give the team about 30 seconds to confer quietly. The captain delivers the final answer. Other teams should stay silent until a ruling is made.
  5. Reveal & rule. Click Reveal Answer to display the correct response. Compare the team's answer to it and decide:
    • Correct or substantially correct → click the green +[points] button under that team.
    • Incorrect → click the red −[points] button under that team (or leave their score unchanged if you prefer a no-penalty rule — pick one approach and stick with it).
    • Partial credit → at your discretion, award a smaller value by clicking +/− on a different point row, or simply award full credit if the key idea is present.
  6. Steal rule (optional). If the controlling team answers incorrectly, you may open the question to the other two teams. The first to raise a hand and answer correctly earns the points; an incorrect steal loses them the points.
  7. Return to the board. Click ← Back to Board. The cell is marked used and play passes to the next team. (Use Skip if you want to leave the question open and revisit it later — it does not mark the cell used.)
  8. Rotation. Control of the board rotates: next team picks the next question. If a team answers a stolen question correctly, control passes to them.
  9. Ending the game. Play continues until all 25 cells are used, or until time runs out. Highest score wins. Use Reset Board to play again with the same teams and scores carried over, or Reset All for a fresh start.
Quick Tips
  • Click a category header at any time to restore that single column.
  • Scores can go negative — useful for the "wrong answer = penalty" rule.
  • The Study Mode button on the title screen lets students review every question and answer before or after the game.
